# Biology revision notes

## Cell structure
Prokaryotic cells lack a nucleus; eukaryotic cells have membrane-bound organelles.

## Photosynthesis
6CO2 + 6H2O + light → C6H12O6 + 6O2. Occurs in chloroplasts (light-dependent and Calvin cycle).

## Genetics
DNA → RNA → protein. Mendel's laws: segregation and independent assortment.

## Ecology
Energy flow through food chains; nutrient cycling; biodiversity indicators.
