# Digital superhighway and offline access

Kenya's digital superhighway aims to expand connectivity, public digital services, and innovation capacity.

## Offline relevance
High mobile-data costs (often KES 25–50 per GB) lock many students and researchers out of cloud AI. Portable offline tools (local LLMs, offline wikis, citation engines) keep research productive without continuous connectivity.

## Policy angles
Last-mile infrastructure, device affordability, digital skills, and open educational resources.
