# Maize smallholder tips

Maize is Kenya's staple cereal. Smallholder yield gaps are driven by seed quality, spacing, soil fertility, and water stress.

## Good agronomy
- Use certified improved seed
- Plant at recommended spacing (75 cm x 25–30 cm)
- Apply basal and top-dress fertilizer based on soil test
- Weed at 2–3 weeks and 5–6 weeks after planting
- Control fall armyworm early

## Harvest & storage
Harvest when cobs dry (moisture ~13–14%). Dry on raised cribs or tarps; store in hermetic bags to reduce weevil losses.
