# Malaria overview

Malaria is a life-threatening disease caused by Plasmodium parasites, transmitted to people through the bites of infected female Anopheles mosquitoes.

## Common symptoms
Fever, chills, headache, body aches, fatigue, nausea or vomiting. In severe cases: confusion, seizures, severe anaemia, respiratory distress.

## Prevention
Insecticide-treated nets (ITNs), indoor residual spraying, antimalarial prophylaxis for travellers, removing standing water near homes, and prompt testing and treatment.

## Kenya context
Malaria remains a major public health concern in lake and coastal endemic counties. Early diagnosis within 24 hours of fever and artemisinin-based combination therapy (ACT) are recommended.
