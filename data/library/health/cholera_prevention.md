# Cholera prevention

Cholera is an acute diarrhoeal infection caused by ingestion of contaminated food or water.

## Prevention
- Drink safe (boiled or treated) water
- Wash hands with soap after toilet use and before eating
- Cook food thoroughly; eat it while hot
- Use improved sanitation facilities
- Oral rehydration salts (ORS) for early treatment of dehydration

## Outbreak response
Community education, chlorination of water sources, case management with ORS/antibiotics, and surveillance.
