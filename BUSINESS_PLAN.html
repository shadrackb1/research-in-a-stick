Research-in-a-Stick (RIS)

Portable offline AI research workstation for Kenyan students · Pilot decision brief · 13 Sep 2026

Recommendation. Fund a 12-month, 3-campus pilot (~1,500 seats). The prototype already runs ChatGPT-style chat fully offline on a USB stick. Next spend buys evidence and content — not a bigger model. Do not mass-produce until weekly active ≥ 35% and one campus converts to a paid license.

Problem & solution

Students face high data costs (project pitch: ~KES 25–50/GB, directional) and unreliable campus connectivity. Cloud AI and Wikipedia burn bundles. RIS is a plug-and-play USB: local LLM (Qwen2.5-0.5B + llama.cpp), knowledge library, document RAG, offline wiki (Kiwix), citations, CSV tools, math/code skills — no cloud, no API keys.

Go-to-market

  • Now (CRIW): live demo, capture pilot LOIs.
  • M1–6: 3 campuses, 1,500 seats, student champions.
  • M7–12: convert 1–2 paid site licenses; publish usage evidence brief.

KPIs for the pilot

  • Boot → first local answer < 60s (p95)
  • Weekly active ≥ 35% of seats; ≥ 90% sessions offline-only
  • Support ≤ 5 tickets / 100 seats / month
  • Renewal intent ≥ 60%

Top risks

  • Adoption — mitigate with champions + curriculum packs.
  • Support load — self-serve START_HERE, check_model.bat.
  • Stick failure — NTFS imaging QC, 10% spare pool.

90-day actions

  2. Image 200 sticks with current stack.
  3. Survey 200 students (WTP, device access, top research tasks).
  4. Expand library packs (methods, past papers).
